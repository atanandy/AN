About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher working at the interface of single-molecule fluorescence spectroscopy, conformational dynamics and membrane biophysics. My research focuses on developing and applying advanced fluorescence-based approaches to understand biomolecular interactions and nanoscale organization in complex biological systems.

My work combines fluorescence correlation spectroscopy (FCS), Time-Tagged Time-Resolved (TTTR) photon acquisition, and graphene-induced energy transfer (GIET) / metal-induced energy transfer (MIET) techniques to investigate molecular diffusion, conformational dynamics, and structural changes at the nanoscale.

A major focus of my research is understanding how biomolecular interactions, including DNA–membrane interactions, influence molecular organization and dynamics. By integrating quantitative spectroscopy, supported lipid membrane systems, and single-molecule approaches, I aim to uncover the physical principles governing biological processes at the molecular level.
