Protein Conformational Dynamics

Understanding how proteins undergo structural fluctuations and transitions across multiple timescales. This includes probing folding–unfolding events, intramolecular rearrangements, and dynamic heterogeneity to reveal how conformational changes regulate biological function.

Energy Transfer in Chromophore Networks

Investigating energy transfer processes among coupled chromophores to understand exciton migration and light-harvesting efficiency. This includes probing Förster resonance energy transfer (FRET) and excitonic interactions in complex nanoscale systems.
