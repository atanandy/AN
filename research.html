Research

Protein Conformational Dynamics

Understanding how proteins undergo structural fluctuations and transitions across multiple timescales. This includes probing folding–unfolding events, intramolecular rearrangements, and dynamic heterogeneity to reveal how conformational changes regulate biological function.

Energy Transfer in Chromophore Networks

Investigating energy transfer processes among coupled chromophores to understand exciton migration and light-harvesting efficiency. This includes probing Förster resonance energy transfer (FRET) and excitonic interactions in complex nanoscale systems.

IDP–Membrane Dynamics

Investigating the interaction of intrinsically disordered proteins (IDPs) with lipid membranes and their dynamic conformational adaptations at interfaces. These studies focus on membrane binding, transient structural changes, and how heterogeneous conformations regulate protein function in complex membrane environments.

Förster Resonance Energy Transfer (FRET)

Förster Resonance Energy Transfer (FRET) is used to probe nanoscale distances and molecular interactions through distance-dependent energy transfer between fluorophores. It enables the investigation of conformational changes, biomolecular interactions, and dynamic processes at the single-molecule level.

Fluorescence Correlation Spectroscopy (FCS)

Fluorescence Correlation Spectroscopy (FCS) is used to quantify molecular diffusion and dynamic processes by analyzing fluorescence intensity fluctuations. The technique provides access to diffusion coefficients, molecular concentrations, and kinetic rates, enabling the study of conformational dynamics and interactions at the single-molecule level.

MIET / GIET

Metal- and Graphene-Induced Energy Transfer (MIET/GIET) are fluorescence lifetime-based techniques that enable nanometer-scale axial positioning of fluorophores relative to surfaces. By exploiting distance-dependent energy transfer to metallic or graphene layers, these methods provide precise information on membrane organization, molecular interactions, and vertical structural dynamics.
